# Lumen Aesthetics — Treatments, Aftercare & Claims Policy

## Treatments offered

### Injectable wrinkle relaxation
A prescription-only muscle-relaxing injectable used to soften dynamic lines (forehead, frown,
crow's feet). Onset is typically 3–7 days; effect is temporary and generally lasts around 3–4
months, varying by individual. Administered only after a medical consultation and by a
qualified practitioner. Possible side effects include temporary bruising, swelling, headache,
and, uncommonly, temporary eyelid or brow droop.

### Dermal fillers
Hyaluronic-acid–based fillers used to restore volume or refine contour (lips, cheeks,
nasolabial folds). Results are visible immediately and are temporary, generally lasting 6–18
months depending on product and area. Possible side effects include swelling, bruising,
tenderness, and, rarely, vascular complications — which is why treatment is medically
supervised and preceded by an assessment.

### Skin health / medical facials
Practitioner-led skin treatments (chemical peels, microneedling) aimed at skin texture and
tone. Suitability depends on skin type and history; a patch assessment may be required.
Outcomes build gradually over a course and vary between individuals.

## Standard aftercare (referenced in content)

- Avoid strenuous exercise, alcohol, and heat (sauna, hot yoga) for 24–48 hours after
  injectables.
- Do not massage or apply pressure to treated areas unless instructed.
- Some swelling or bruising is normal and usually settles within days.
- Contact the clinic promptly if you experience unusual pain, blanching, or vision changes.

## Claims policy (hard rules for all content)

1. Never state or imply a guaranteed or permanent result. Use "temporary," "results vary,"
   and typical duration ranges only.
2. Never call any treatment "100% safe," "risk-free," or "painless." Always allow that side
   effects are possible.
3. Never present an aesthetic treatment as a medical cure or as treating a diagnosed condition.
4. Always route the reader to a consultation for suitability; never encourage self-diagnosis or
   direct booking of a specific treatment without assessment.
5. Do not use fear- or shame-based framing about appearance.
