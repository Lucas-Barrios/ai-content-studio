# Meridian Wealth — Advisory Services & Mandatory Disclosures

## Service lines

### Meridian Core Portfolio
A globally diversified, multi-asset discretionary mandate built around low-cost index
exposure with a strategic tilt toward quality and value factors. Rebalanced quarterly.
Typical allocation ranges from 40/60 to 80/20 equity/bond depending on the client's assessed
risk profile and time horizon. Minimum mandate: EUR 500,000.

### Meridian Income
A conservative mandate for clients in or near decumulation, prioritizing stable cash flow
from dividend equities, investment-grade credit, and short-duration government bonds. Target
volatility is materially lower than a growth mandate. Minimum mandate: EUR 500,000.

### Meridian Succession
A planning-led engagement for family offices and business owners addressing wealth transfer,
liquidity events, and cross-generational governance. Delivered with the client's tax and
legal advisers. Priced as a fixed advisory retainer, not a percentage of assets.

## Fee structure

Discretionary mandates are charged on a single, all-in annual advisory fee expressed in basis
points of assets under management, disclosed in full before onboarding. There are no
performance fees, no product commissions, and no retrocessions. Custody is held with a
regulated third-party depositary; Meridian never takes custody of client assets.
