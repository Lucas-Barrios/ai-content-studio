# Meridian Wealth — Brand & Voice Guidelines

Meridian Wealth is a boutique wealth advisory firm headquartered in Frankfurt, serving
private clients and family offices across the DACH region. All client-facing content is a
financial promotion and must meet the firm's compliance standard before publication.

## Positioning

Meridian Wealth helps clients preserve and grow capital through disciplined, evidence-based
portfolio construction. The firm competes on rigor and transparency, not on performance
claims. The core promise is clarity: clients always understand what they own and why.

## Audience

- Private clients with EUR 500k–5m in investable assets
- Family offices and business owners planning succession
- Professionals approaching retirement who want a second opinion on an existing portfolio

Readers are financially literate but time-poor and skeptical of hype. They respond to
specificity, evidence, and candor about risk.

## Voice

- Precise and sober. Every sentence earns its place.
- Evidence-led. Claims are backed by a mechanism or a source, never by adjectives.
- Candid about risk. Downside is stated plainly, never buried.
- Calm authority. Confident without being promotional.

## Approved terms

diversified, evidence-based, disciplined, long-term, risk-adjusted, transparent fee
structure, capital preservation, suitability, time horizon, rebalancing, may, historically

## Banned terms (financial-promotion compliance — never use)

- "guaranteed" / "guarantee"
- "risk-free" / "no risk" / "safe returns"
- "beat the market" / "outperform the market"
- "sure thing" / "can't lose" / "double your money"
- "get rich" / "high returns" (unqualified)
- Any specific return figure stated as a promise rather than a historical, sourced range

## Mandatory compliance stance

- Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results — this idea must be present
  wherever performance is discussed.
- The value of investments can fall as well as rise; clients may get back less than invested.
- Content is marketing, not personal advice; recommendations require a suitability assessment.
- No comparative superiority claim ("the best," "number one") without a dated, cited source.

## CTA conventions

Primary CTA is a "Book a portfolio review" conversation, never a product purchase. Content
educates toward a suitability conversation; it never instructs the reader to buy.
